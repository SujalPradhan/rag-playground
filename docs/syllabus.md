## What this course is about
BIO 101 introduces the basic ideas of living systems: cells, energy, genetics and ecosystems. No prior biology is required. By the end of the course students should be able to explain how cells get and use energy, describe how traits are inherited, and read a simple scientific paper.

## Weekly schedule
- Week 1: What is life? Characteristics of living things
- Week 2: Cells, organelles and the cell membrane
- Week 3: Enzymes and metabolism
- Week 4: Photosynthesis (Chapter 5)
- Week 5: Cellular respiration
- Week 6: Mid-semester exam (Wednesday, Week 6, 10:00 am, Hall B)
- Week 7: DNA structure and replication
- Week 8: Mendelian genetics
- Week 9: Evolution and natural selection
- Week 10: Ecosystems and energy flow
- Week 11: Human impact on the environment
- Week 12: Revision week and final project presentations
- Week 13: Final exam (date published by the exam office)

## Labs
