# Student FAQ — Riverside Community College (fictional sample)

## Fees and payments
**When is the semester fee due?** The tuition fee for the autumn semester is due by 15 August. A late payment fee of 500 rupees is added after that date. Students can pay in two instalments if they apply to the accounts office before 1 August.

**Is there a scholarship?** Merit scholarships cover up to 50% of tuition for students with an A average in the previous semester. Need-based bursaries are available through the Student Support Office. Applications close on 30 September.

## Library
**What are the library hours?** The library is open 8:00 am to 9:00 pm on weekdays and 10:00 am to 5:00 pm on Saturdays. It is closed on Sundays and public holidays. During exam weeks it stays open until midnight.

**How many books can I borrow?** Students can borrow up to 5 books for 14 days. Reserve-collection books can only be borrowed for 2 hours and cannot leave the library. The fine for late returns is 10 rupees per book per day.

## Exams
**What do I bring to an exam?** Your student ID card, blue or black pens, and a basic calculator if allowed. Phones must be switched off and left in your bag at the front of the hall.

**What if I am ill on exam day?** Inform the exam office by email on the same day and submit a medical certificate within 5 working days. You may then be allowed to sit a deferred exam.

**Where can I see my exam timetable?** Final exam timetables are published on the learning portal four weeks before the exam period.

## Hostel
**What are the hostel rules on visitors?** Visitors are allowed in common rooms between 9:00 am and 8:00 pm. Overnight guests are not permitted. The hostel gate closes at 10:30 pm.

## Wellbeing and support
**Is there counselling?** Free and confidential counselling is available at the Student Wellbeing Centre, Monday to Friday, 9:00 am to 5:00 pm. Book through the portal or walk in.

**Who do I contact about disability support?** The Accessibility Office arranges extra exam time, note-takers and accessible materials. Contact them at the start of the semester so arrangements are in place before exams.
