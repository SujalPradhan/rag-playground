# BIO 101 Grading and Assessment Policy

## How your grade is calculated
- Lab reports (6 reports): 30%
- Weekly online quizzes: 10%
- Mid-semester exam: 20%
- Final project (poster and 5-minute presentation): 15%
- Final exam: 25%

## Grade boundaries
- A: 85% and above
- B: 70% to 84%
- C: 55% to 69%
- D: 45% to 54%
- F: below 45%

A minimum of 40% in the final exam is required to pass the course, regardless of the overall total.

## Late submission penalty
Lab reports and the final project are due at 11:59 pm on the due date through the learning portal. Late work loses 10% of the marks available for each day it is late, for up to 3 days. Work submitted more than 3 days late receives zero, unless an extension was approved in advance.

## Extensions
Extensions are granted for illness, family emergencies or approved college events. Request an extension by emailing the instructor before the deadline, with supporting evidence such as a medical note. Extensions are normally for up to 7 days.

## Weekly quizzes
Quizzes open every Friday at 9:00 am and close on Sunday at 11:59 pm. Each quiz has 10 questions and two attempts are allowed; the higher score counts. The lowest two quiz scores of the semester are dropped.

## Re-grading
If you believe a lab report or exam was marked incorrectly, submit a re-grade request through the portal within 7 days of the marks being released. The whole piece of work may be re-marked, so the mark can go up or down.

## Academic integrity
All submitted work must be your own. Copying from classmates, websites or AI tools without acknowledgement is treated as plagiarism. A first offence results in zero for that assignment; a second offence is referred to the Academic Conduct Committee. You may use AI tools to check grammar or explain concepts, but you must state how you used them at the end of your report.
